About the forum

The Doctoral Research Forum is a yearly event of the ILS. It focuses on motivating Ph.D. students to participate in innovative research through the lens of information science, library science, social sciences, policy studies, and other disciplines.

Current PhD students may present either completed or in-progress research. This is a chance to share work at any stage of completion (please do not be shy in sharing work in progress), and a great opportunity to hear what our fellow colleagues are up to. The presentation would be about 10-15 minutes.
