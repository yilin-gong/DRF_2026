Opening keynote

9:30 – 10:00

Joshua Blumenstock

Chancellor's Professor at the UC Berkeley School of Information and the Goldman School of Public Policy

Joshua Blumenstock is a professor at the UC Berkeley School of Information, the director of the Global Opportunity Lab, and the faculty co-director of the Center for Effective Global Action.

His research combines methods from computer science and economics, with a focus on how AI and other technologies can better address the needs of poor and vulnerable populations. His work appears in Science, Nature and PNAS, as well as economics journals and computer science conferences.
